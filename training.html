TRAIN SMART

Training Tips

Small habits lead to big improvements

01

Consistent Training is Key

Regular short sessions is better than a few big sessions

02

Remember to train both feet

Practise with your weaker foot too, you can become balanced and very unpredicatble

03

Focus most on technique

Get the basics right, then add more speed and power.
